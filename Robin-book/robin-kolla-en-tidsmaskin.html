En rymdraket!

Chapter 1 · A Rocket!
Sida 6

Molly och Elton bor i precis likadana radhus. Annars är det mesta olika.

Hos Elton är det ordning. I hans familj vill alla tävla. Mamma och pappa spelar tennis och jobbar mycket. Storebror Sandor spelar fotboll, precis som Elton.

Elton älskar att vinna. Därför är det synd att alla andra i hans familj också älskar att vinna. Men det är bra att Elton är kompis med Molly, för de har en tävling som är svår. I tävlingen kommer de på saker som den andra ska göra. Just nu står det 1–0 till Elton.

Molly and Elton live in exactly identical row houses. Apart from that, they're mostly different.

At Elton's house, everything is orderly. Everyone in his family loves to compete. Mom and Dad play tennis and work a lot. Big brother Sandor plays football, just like Elton.

Elton loves to win. Too bad, then, that everyone else in his family loves to win too. But it's lucky Elton is friends with Molly, because the two of them have a game that's hard — they take turns thinking up things for the other one to do. Right now the score is 1–0 to Elton.

Jo, förresten! Robin vill städa. Vilken tur att Mollys pappa är uppfinnare. Förut uppfann han Robin. Nu har han uppfunnit en… Just det, det är ju hemligt.

Det skramlar och borras inne i verkstan. Robin släpar hemliga saker fram och tillbaka. Elton och Molly försöker titta in genom fönstret.

Robin rullar ut från verkstan.

– Det är inte han som ska ut i rymden. Det är jag. Men det där hörde ni inte heller från mig.

At Molly's house, it's messy. Nobody here wants to clean — not Dad, not Molly, not Molly's big sister Ines either.

Oh, actually! Robin wants to clean. Lucky that Molly's dad is an inventor. He invented Robin once. Now he's invented a… right, that's a secret.

There's rattling and drilling going on inside the workshop. Robin drags secret things back and forth. Elton and Molly try to peek in through the window.

Robin rolls out of the workshop.

"It's a little space rocket, but it's going to fly just fine," says Robin. "But I'm not allowed to say that."

"How's Dad going to fit inside the rocket?"

"He's not the one going into space. I am. But you didn't hear that from me either."

Elton får bråttom hem till Molly. Han kryper genom hålet i buskarna.

In the middle of the night, Elton wakes up to a noise outside the window. He looks out and sees that Molly's dad's workshop is lit up. He notices something strange — the roof is opening!

Elton hurries over to Molly's house. He crawls through the gap in the bushes.

Elton rings the doorbell. Molly opens the door, yawning.

"Hi," she says sleepily. "Are you sleepwalking?"

Elton points at the workshop and talks as fast as he can.

"The roof is open — what if Robin's about to fly off in the rocket!"

Molly instantly wakes up and races off after Elton.

Elton och Molly är så nyfikna att de går in i verkstan fast de inte får.

Det är verkligen en riktig raket! Mollys pappa står på en stege för att hjälpa Robin. Han får syn på dem.

– Oj, är ni här? säger han glatt. Jag har uppfunnit en raket!

Elton and Molly are so curious that they go into the workshop even though they're not allowed to.

It really is a real rocket! Molly's dad is standing on a ladder helping Robin. He spots them.

"Oh, are you here?" he says cheerfully. "I've invented a rocket!"

"Bye then, see you soon," says Robin, waving.

Dad closes the hatch on the rocket and climbs down.

"We need to go outside now, the engines are about to start."

De ser hur raketen långsamt lyfter och flyger upp mot himlen. På en sekund är den uppe i rymden och borta.

– Kolla! Den fungerar, ropar pappa.

Elton, Molly, and Molly's dad go outside from the workshop. There's a loud rumbling, sounding like lots of airplanes. Molly's big sister Ines comes out and asks what's going on.

They watch the rocket slowly lift off and fly up toward the sky. In a second it's up in space and gone.

"Look! It works!" Dad shouts.

They're so happy they start jumping up and down. Ines just stands there, gaping.

Elton och Judit

Chapter 2 · Elton and Judit

Elton and Molly have started second grade. The door says 2A. They hang up their jackets outside the classroom. Then they say good morning to Vida.

In first grade, Elton sat next to Molly. Now he sits next to Judit. Elton likes sitting next to her — she also loves writing and doing math. Judit says funny things that make Elton laugh.

"We need to work quietly," says Vida.

Molly sits next to Danilo, looking grumpy.

After recess, Elton and Judit have to drink a lot of water. Their hair is sweaty and their knees are dirty.

Elton thinks it was a good match. Judit and Elton beat Hamid and Pella.

"We're the best," says Elton.

Elton and Judit want to. Elton turns to Molly and Danilo.

"Want to join in?" he asks.

Elton and Molly usually walk home from school together. But today Molly just walks off. She doesn't even say bye.

Elton has to run after her. He wants things to be normal with Molly again. Then he remembers their game. Now it's Elton's turn to decide what Molly has to do.

"You remember the game, right?" he asks. "When Robin gets back, you have to ride in the space rocket."

"That's a dumb game," says Molly.

"It's exciting. You're the one who came up with it."

He wants Molly to be happy again. But Molly just keeps walking ahead with quick steps.

– Det kanske är roligare för dig att vara med Judit. Hon gillar också att tävla, säger Molly.

It's boring walking with someone who's grumpy. Elton makes funny faces. He tickles Molly and tells silly stories.

"Maybe it's more fun for you to be with Judit. She likes competing too," says Molly.

"I want everyone to be included. I'm friends with Danilo, Hamid, Pella, Judit, and you," says Elton.

"But it's the two of us who walk to school together," says Molly.

"And are neighbors," says Elton.

"And have an exciting game," says Molly.

Elton and Molly scan the sky. Up there is Robin.

"Will you dare?" asks Elton.

"I dare anything," Molly answers.

Raket-Robin

Chapter 3 · Rocket-Robin
Sida 16
Lyssna på svenskaNative Swedish audio

Molly sitter i sin säng och tittar på stjärnorna. Hon borde sova för länge sen, men hon har många tankar i huvudet.

Hon tänker på hur det är i skolan. Det blir ofta bråkigt när Judit är med. Molly tycker att det är svårt att veta om Elton fortfarande är hennes bästa vän. Hon kramar om kudden. Åh, vad hon saknar Robin.

Just då ser Molly ett stjärnfall på himlen. Det är en konstig stjärna för den flyger rakt mot hennes hus!

Molly sits in her bed looking at the stars. She should have been asleep long ago, but her head is full of thoughts.

She thinks about what school is like. It often gets messy when Judit's around. Molly finds it hard to know if Elton is still her best friend. She hugs her pillow. Oh, how she misses Robin.

Just then Molly sees a shooting star in the sky. It's a strange star, because it's flying straight toward her house!

Molly runs in to her dad. It's messy in here as usual. Molly steps on something hard.

"Ow!" she shouts.

"What?" asks Dad, sitting up in bed.

"It's a star!" Molly shouts. "No, I mean a rocket! No, I mean Robin!"

Dad springs up and looks at his strange watch.

"That's wrong. Robin is supposed to land in exactly three days and fourteen seconds."

Just then Ines comes running in, pointing toward the garden.

"The rocket! Robin! Come on!"

All three of them rush out of the room.

Sida 18
Lyssna på svenskaNative Swedish audio

– Fridens på er, människor! Får jag presentera Raket-Robin, er nya superhjälte!

Molly förstår ingenting, Robin pratar väldigt konstigt.

– Plutanien är en superhjälteplanet.

– Du kan inte ha hunnit till en annan planet. Det är omöjligt, säger pappa.

They run into the workshop just as the rocket lands. Molly's heart is pounding hard in her chest. The rocket's hatch opens and Robin flies out with a cape around its neck.

"Peace be with you, humans! May I present Rocket-Robin, your new superhero!"

Everyone wants to hug Robin. Dad feels the cape.

"Where did you find this?" he asks. "It wasn't in the rocket."

"Correct you are, you red-haired inventor — I got it on Plutania."

Molly doesn't understand anything; Robin is talking very strangely.

"Plutania is a planet of superheroes."

"You can't possibly have made it to another planet. That's impossible," says Dad.

"I pressed the yellow button. Swoosh, it went. Then I was there."

Sida 19
Lyssna på svenskaNative Swedish audio

Robin och Molly sitter i raketen. Pappa och Ines står på stegen och tittar in.

– Var är knappen? frågar pappa.

Robin pekar på en gul knapp.

– Jaha, den ska man ju trycka på om man vill lyssna på musik, säger pappa.

Robin startar en dator i raketen. Det dyker upp några figurer på skärmen som vinkar.

– Hjälp! ropar Molly och Ines.

– Superhjältar, förklarar Robin.

– Fridens på er, jordbarn, ropar superhjältarna.

– Vart ska du? frågar Molly.

– Rädda någon, såklart!

Och så är Robin borta.

Robin and Molly sit in the rocket. Dad and Ines stand on the ladder, looking in.

"Where's the button?" asks Dad.

Robin points at a yellow button.

"Ah, you press that one if you want to listen to music," says Dad.

"But I ended up with the superheroes."

Robin starts up a computer in the rocket. A few figures pop up on the screen, waving.

"Help!" shout Molly and Ines.

"Superheroes," Robin explains.

"Peace be with you, Earth children," the superheroes shout.

"Not everyone has a superhero at home," says Robin, flying off.

"Where are you going?" asks Molly.

"To save someone, obviously!"

And with that, Robin is gone.

Molly and Robin go out to meet Elton. Elton waves cheerfully when he spots Robin.

"Was it exciting in space?" he asks.

Robin shows off its cape.

"I've been to Plutania."

"What's that?"

"A planet of superheroes," Molly explains.

Elton looks surprised.

"How did you have time for that?" he asks.

"Boy with the bangs, I'm Rocket-Robin, I have time for everything."

"You're talking strangely. My name is Elton, you know that."

"That's how superheroes talk," says Robin.

The class has started working on space. Vida explains that the sun is a star and that there are eight planets circling the sun.

"But there's only one planet in our solar system where we know there's life, and that's Earth," she says.

Molly and Elton look at each other in surprise. Where on earth could Robin have been?

Vida talks and talks about the planets and the sun. Molly finds it hard to listen. Finally it's time to draw. The children are going to make the planets that circle the sun. When they're done, Vida will help hang them from the ceiling.

Molly, Elton, Judit, and Danilo work on the planets together. Some are much bigger than Earth, and some are smaller.

"Imagine, we live on the only planet where there's life," says Judit.

Molly thinks about Plutania, but she can't reveal anything, because she's promised Dad she wouldn't.

"I think there's life on other planets too," she says.

"I don't think so," says Judit.

Vida comes over and looks at their work.

"Couldn't there be life on other planets?" Molly asks.

"Not as far as we know," says Vida.

But we know, Molly thinks.

"I wasn't on Mars! I was much farther away," says Robin.
