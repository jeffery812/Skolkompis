A garage full of motorcycles 🏍️ and cars 🚗 — we only know how many vehicles and how many wheels in total. Can you figure out which is which? Let's crack the case with the Wheel-Counting Trick!

The Wheel-Counting Trick

Let's learn the trick with a small example: pretend every vehicle is a motorcycle, then see how many wheels are missing — that's how we spot the hidden cars!

Quick Tip · The Wheel-Counting Steps

  1. Pretend every vehicle is a motorcycle: guessed wheels = total vehicles × 2
  2. Subtract guessed wheels from the real wheel count to find the extra wheels
  3. Extra wheels ÷ 2 = number of cars (each car has 2 more wheels than a motorcycle)
  4. Total vehicles − cars = number of motorcycles
